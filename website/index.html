Built-in Local AI

Amalgam includes a local AI runtime. Optional models are installed from Settings > AI after setup:

  • Brain: Qwen3-VL 8B for chat, coding, and reasoning
  • Vision: Live screen analysis — AI describes your Minecraft world in real-time
  • Art: FLUX 2 Klein 4B for generating textures, logos, and artwork
  • Build Mode: Generate KubeJS scripts, datapacks, and resource packs with one click
  • Checkpoints: Every AI change is reversible — undo instantly

No Ollama, LM Studio, ComfyUI, or cloud API is required for the local runtime. Cloud providers can be configured separately if you choose.
