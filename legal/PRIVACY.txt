AMALGAM PRIVACY POLICY — RELEASE-READY DRAFT
=============================================
Status: DRAFT — OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLIC RELEASE

Version 1.0.0 — Amalgam Launcher

This document is a release-ready draft of the Amalgam privacy policy. It
has NOT been reviewed or approved by legal counsel. Distribution or public
release requires owner/legal review and approval of this text.

1. OVERVIEW
   Amalgam processes two distinct categories of data:
   - LOCAL AI PROCESSING — performed on your own computer.
   - ONLINE FEATURES — features that communicate with remote services.

   This distinction matters. Local AI never leaves your PC; online features
   transmit data to the services you use.

2. LOCAL AI PROCESSING
   Amalgam's built-in AI (chat, coding, reasoning, vision, and art) runs
   locally on your device using on-device models. Conversations, project
   files, screenshots, and generated content used by the local AI are
   processed on your machine and are not transmitted to Amalgam servers.
   Model downloads during installation come from the sources described in
   the installer; the downloaded models themselves are not uploaded.

3. ONLINE FEATURES
   The following features communicate over the network and may transmit
   data to third parties:
   - Microsoft account authentication and Minecraft entitlement checks
   - Supabase-backed services (Essentials: friends, presence, invites,
     sessions)
   - Mod and content providers (e.g., Modrinth, CurseForge)
   - Software updates and AI component downloads
   - Cloud hosting features, where available
   - Diagnostics you explicitly choose to send

   Data sent to these services is governed by their respective policies and
   by the specific feature you are using. Amalgam never claims that these
   features are local-only.

4. DATA WE STORE LOCALLY
   Amalgam stores local data on your device, including:
   - profiles, worlds, servers, and backups,
   - settings and configuration,
   - AI conversations and checkpoints,
   - cached content and logs.

   This data stays in your user profile unless you remove it.

5. CREDENTIALS
   Microsoft sign-in uses Microsoft's secure device-code flow. Amalgam
   never asks for or stores your Microsoft password. Saved tokens are
   protected for the current Windows account using DPAPI. Provider and AI
   credentials are kept on your device.

6. ANALYTICS AND DIAGNOSTICS
   Optional diagnostics remain local unless you explicitly opt in to send
   them. We do not sell your personal data.

7. CHILDREN
   Amalgam is not directed at children. If you are under the age of
   majority in your jurisdiction, use Amalgam only with a parent or
   guardian's involvement.

8. CHANGES
   We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes
   acceptance of the updated policy.

9. CONTACT
   For privacy questions, contact Amalgam support.
