Version 1.0.0 — Amalgam Launcher

1. WHAT AMALGAM AI IS
   Amalgam includes local AI capabilities for chat, reasoning, coding,
   project analysis, live vision, and art generation. The AI models and
   inference runtime are installed on your computer. No cloud API is
   required for core AI features.

2. AI OUTPUT IS NOT GUARANTEED
   AI models may generate incorrect, incomplete, biased, or unsuitable
   output. AI-generated code, configuration, recipes, datapacks, and
   content should be reviewed before use in important projects. Amalgam
   provides checkpoints and undo so you can revert AI changes.

3. AI FILE MODIFICATIONS
   When Agent Mode is enabled, Amalgam AI may create, edit, or remove
   files inside your approved AI Profile workspaces to fulfill your
   requests. You control which actions require confirmation. Amalgam AI
   does not modify files outside your approved AI Profile sandbox.

4. LOCAL PROCESSING
   Local AI inference runs on your PC. Your conversations, project files,
   and generated content used by the local AI stay on your machine and are
   not uploaded to Amalgam servers.

5. ONLINE COMPONENTS
   The following Amalgam features are online and are NOT local:
   - downloading AI models/components during installation,
   - Microsoft authentication,
   - Supabase-backed Essentials features,
   - mod/content provider searches,
   - updates,
   - cloud hosting features.

6. MODEL LICENSES
   Amalgam AI uses third-party models and runtimes subject to their own
   licenses, including Qwen (Apache-2.0), FLUX (FLUX.2-dev Non-Commercial
   License), llama.cpp (MIT), and stable-diffusion.cpp (MIT). Full
   notices are installed with the product under the licenses directory.

7. REVIEW AND ACCOUNTABILITY
   You are responsible for reviewing AI output before publishing,
   distributing, or relying on it. Amalgam is not liable for content
   generated by AI on your behalf.

8. CONTACT
   For questions about Amalgam AI, contact Amalgam support.
