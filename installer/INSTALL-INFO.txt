PRE-INSTALLATION INFORMATION
============================

Welcome to Amalgam Launcher 1.0.0 — the unified Minecraft launcher
with built-in local AI for coding, vision, and art generation.

WHAT AMALGAM INSTALLS
---------------------
* Amalgam Launcher (required)
* Native support library
* Java bridges for supported Minecraft versions
* Bedrock client package
* Branding assets and release manifests
* Legal documents (Terms, Privacy, AI Terms, Third-Party Notices)

AMALGAM AI (optional, downloads after setup)
---------------------------------------------
AI models are not downloaded by the installer. After setup, install them
from Settings > AI when you choose:

  AI Brain (Qwen3-VL-8B):   ~4.7 GiB
  AI Vision (mmproj):       ~0.7 GiB
  AI Art (FLUX.2):          ~2.3 GiB
  AI Art Encoder (Qwen3):   ~2.3 GiB
  AI Decoder:                ~0.2 GiB

  Total download:           ~10.2 GiB
  Installed size:            ~10.2 GiB

Amalgam AI runs locally. No cloud API or external software is required.

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS
--------------------
  Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
  Vulkan-compatible GPU (recommended for AI)
  16 GB RAM (32 GB recommended for full AI)
  4 GB VRAM (8+ GB recommended for AI art)
  25 GB free disk space (with AI)

UNINSTALLING
------------
The uninstaller removes the application files by default. It then asks
whether to also permanently remove Amalgam AI models, profiles, modpacks,
worlds, servers, backups, and downloaded content for this Windows account.
Choose No to keep that user data for a later reinstall.
